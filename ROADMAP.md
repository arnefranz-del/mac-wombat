Use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/arnefranz-del/mac-wombat/issues) for individual tasks and [milestones](https://github.com/arnefranz-del/mac-wombat/milestones) for progress. Dates are review points. A release requires the relevant acceptance checks to pass.

## Scope

The source preview covers Mac Wombat. Home network monitoring, router logins and device history are separate research work. For Mac Wombat, the first priority is a trustworthy build and predictable data handling. Contributions to documentation, accessibility and compatibility are welcome.
