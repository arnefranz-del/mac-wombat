Rates in the menu bar

Keep an eye on current download and upload rates. Choose from three layouts to control how much space Mac Wombat takes in your menu bar.

Usage over time

Browse your history by hour, day, week, month, year, or all recorded time. Sleep and other unobserved intervals appear as gaps.

On-demand measurements

Record activity during a transfer. Set a timer or stop manually, then save the result in the app. Export your regular usage history as CSV.

What gets counted

Mac Wombat reads counters from active Wi-Fi and Ethernet interfaces. That can include local network traffic and VPN traffic carried on those interfaces.

Measurement limits and per-app traffic

Totals reflect observed interface traffic and are unsuitable for billing. The Programme tab shows a limited snapshot of per-app traffic. It keeps no per-app history.

How Mac Wombat measures traffic ↗

Your history stays on your Mac.

History and saved measurements live in a local SQLite database. Mac Wombat works without an account, telemetry, or cloud sync. A CSV file is created when you choose to export it.
