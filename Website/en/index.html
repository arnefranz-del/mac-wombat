Live in the menu bar

Download and upload rates from active Wi-Fi and Ethernet interfaces.

Usage history

Local history by hour, day and longer periods. Sleep and interruptions appear as gaps.

Measurements and CSV

Start a measurement, save the result and export your usage history as CSV.

Mac Wombat reads counters on physical interfaces. Local traffic and VPN traffic carried by those interfaces can be included. Usage totals are not billing records. Per-app figures are a snapshot.

Your history stays on your Mac.

Wombat stores usage history locally. There is no Wombat account, telemetry or cloud sync.
